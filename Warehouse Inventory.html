Recommendations

1

Transfer stock first

Move stock between warehouses before buying more. About 3,330 units (Rp1.45B) needed.

2

Review open POs

295 open POs (Rp1.29B) cover products with 1+ year of stock.

3

Dynamic reorder levels

Set levels per product and warehouse using demand and lead time.

  • Reduce slow-moving stock: promotions, bundles, supplier returns.
  • Evaluate long lead-time suppliers: negotiate or find alternatives.
  • Balance warehouse utilization: use Jakarta as a benchmark.
  • Monitor routinely: weekly stock status, monthly KPIs.

Figures are estimates of need, not realized savings.

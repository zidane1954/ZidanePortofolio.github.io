Coffee Shop Sales Performance Dashboard

From Business Problem to Data-Driven Insight

I built a one-page dashboard to track sales for a coffee shop chain: 149,116 transactions, 3 stores, January–June 2023. It shows what to prepare, what to stock, and when to promote.

Tools: Excel, Looker Studio

Background & Problem

The chain had no single view of sales across stores and time. Without it, management struggled to:

  • Production: match barista staffing to the morning rush (7–10 AM).
  • Inventory: stock the right products and categories.
  • Marketing: lift the $4.70 average spend, especially in slow afternoons.

Objectives & Questions

Build a one-page dashboard that answers four sales questions, clearly and without clutter.

#Question
1What are the top 5 products by quantity sold?
2Which day of the week has the most transactions?
3Which hour of the day has the highest sales?
4What are the top 5 categories by sales each month?

Key Findings

1. Sales more than doubled

  • +119% from February to June.
  • +6% June vs May, so growth is slowing.
  • $698.8K total revenue in six months.

2. Coffee and Tea drive revenue

  • Coffee 38.6% + Tea 28.1% of revenue.
  • Top 5 categories make up 94.6%.
  • Stores are evenly matched: 32.9%–33.8% each.

3. Top products by quantity

RankProductUnits
1Brewed Chai Tea26.25K
2Gourmet Brewed Coffee25.97K
3Barista Espresso24.94K
4Brewed Black Tea17.46K
5Hot Chocolate17.46K

Most units ≠ most revenue: Barista Espresso earns the most ($91.4K) but ranks third in units.

4. Peak time

  • Peak hour: 10 AM, with the morning rush as the busiest window.
  • Busiest day: Friday (21.7K transactions), but all weekdays are evenly busy.

Recommendations

InsightAction
Sales peak in the morningFull barista coverage from 6:30 AM and finish prep before 7:00 AM. Add afternoon "Happy Hour" bundles (coffee + bakery, 1–4 PM).
Chai Tea, Gourmet Coffee, and Espresso lead in volumeRaise safety stock for these drinks (especially Thursday–Friday). Bundle with bakery to lift average spend.
June is the highest month, February the lowestScale up purchasing and seasonal campaigns before April–June. Use loyalty rewards or seasonal drinks in January–February.

Dashboard

One page, four KPIs, four charts. Each chart answers one question.

  • KPIs: Total Revenue (698.8K), Total Orders (149.1K), Quantity Sold (214.5K), Average Spend ($4.70).
  • Filters: date range and store location update every chart.
  • Q1: Top 5 products: horizontal bar.
  • Q2: Busiest day: column chart.
  • Q3: Peak hour: line chart.
  • Q4: Top 5 categories per month: stacked column.

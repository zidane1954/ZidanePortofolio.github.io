So, who exactly am I?

I am a final-year Mathematics student at Universitas Gadjah Mada with a strong interest in data analytics and the use of data to support informed decision-making. My academic background in Mathematics has shaped my analytical and structured approach to problem-solving. Beyond academics, my active involvement in various student organizations and event committees has strengthened my skills in leadership, team management, strategic planning, and fact-based decision-making.

This interest has motivated me to continuously explore and deepen my knowledge of data analytics through self-directed learning. I have been developing my skills in SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, and Looker, applying them through hands-on projects. From cleaning and processing raw data to analyzing patterns and trends and building interactive dashboards, I strive to transform data into meaningful, actionable insights that support better decision-making.

To me, data analytics is more than just numbers and visualizations. It is about uncovering patterns, understanding problems, and turning information into a foundation for better decisions. I enjoy exploring new questions, testing assumptions through data, and continuously strengthening my analytical and technical skills.

Beyond the world of data, I enjoy mystery films and television series that challenge me to connect clues, recognize patterns, and draw conclusions. This interest reflects my curiosity and enthusiasm for exploring data and discovering new perspectives.

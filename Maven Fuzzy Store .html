Monthly sessions

Traffic grew from March 2012 to December 2014.

1 → 4

From Mr. Fuzzy alone to four different products.

Did more traffic and more products lead to better sales and marketing? Only about 1 in 15 sessions ends in a purchase, so small gains matter.

Data & Method

6

Tables

0

Duplicates in key tables

UTM

Missing traffic sources

Dropped first visits with no source. Filled repeat visits from the first visit (Fill Down).

No ad cost data, so ROAS cannot be calculated.
